    Data: Loop Habit Tracker CSV export, normalized by scripts/normalize.py.

    A check-in is a day marked done. A habit is on track on a day when it’s done, or when Loop fills the day in because that week’s or month’s target is already met (for “twice a week”, two check-ins cover the rest of the week). Streaks count on-track days in a row, as Loop does. Strength is Loop’s habit score: it rises with every check-in and fades with every missed one, 100% = a firmly held habit. Days before a habit was started don’t count against it.
